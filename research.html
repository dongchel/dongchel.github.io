Quantum & Precision Metrology

I work across theory and experiment. Starting from first principles, I find what fundamentally limits a system, then build what it takes to reach that limit: the optics, mechanics, and electronics, and the control and instrumentation that tie them together.

  • Macroscopic quantum mechanics
  • Theoretical tools for quantum metrology
  • Frequency comb metrology
